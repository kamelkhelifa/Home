Alhamdulillah, and may blessings and peace be upon the Messenger of Allah, upon his family, his Companions and all who follow him.
To proceed:
Here, in sha' Allah, we mention a number of charitable projects that carry a great reward, require little effort and bring benefit to others, yet which many people overlook or neglect. Among them:

1- Visiting hospitals and seeing the condition of the sick, so that you take heed yourself and remind others.
2- Visiting orphanages, homes for the elderly and the disabled, and others among the "forgotten" people.
3- Donating blood.
4- Visiting graveyards to take heed.

And here are some useful guidelines that help in achieving what is hoped for from these projects:
1- Doing them regularly, by setting aside time for them, for example once every week, every two weeks or every month. In the hadith: "The most beloved of deeds to Allah are those done consistently, even if they are few."
2- Seizing the opportunity to remind people of Allah and the Last Day, and to offer admonition and guidance, by bringing along some booklets, leaflets and other da'wah materials; for people in such places (hospitals and the like …) are at their most in need of sincere advice and at their most ready to respond.
