Export student list

  1. Assuming you are logged into GaelLearn (moodle) and in your course site, click Grades.
  2. At the Choose An Action menu, locate Export and choose one of the following --> Open Document Spreadsheet, Plain Text File, Excel Spreadsheet, or XML file. (For this example, choose Excel Spreadsheet.)
  3. You will be in the Export To Excel Spreadsheet screen. Choose the options you want in your excel spreadsheet. Most of the default settings are fine.
  4. Click Submit.
  5. At the next screen, click Download.
  6. At this point, your computer will most likely give you a dialog box asking what you want to do with the downloaded item. Most of the time it will ask if you want to Save File or Open.
  7. The result should be an excel spreadsheet that includes the previously selected data from the Grades page in GaelLearn (moodle). Since this excel spreadsheet is downloaded to your computer, you can edit and save this spreadsheet, as needed.
