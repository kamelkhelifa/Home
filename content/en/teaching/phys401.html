Syllabus

Course description

This introductory course in quantum mechanics covers the basics of quantum mechanics and its applications.

Learning outcomes

Upon completion, successful students will be able to:

  1. understand the basic properties of wave-functions and measurements in QM.
  2. appreciate the role and significance of operators and eigenvalue problems in QM.
  3. solve Schrödinger equation in one-dimension for potential wells and harmonic oscillator.
  4. solve Schrödinger equation in three-dimensions.
  5. demonstrate an understanding of the angular momentum in QM.
  6. utilise QM to understand central potential problems and describe the hydrogen atom.
  7. appreciate the spin of a particle.
