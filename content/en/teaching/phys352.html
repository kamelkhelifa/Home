Syllabus

Course description

This course provides the undergraduate students necessary mathematical per-requisites to study core physics courses.

Learning outcomes

Upon completion, successful students will be able to:

  1. use gamma and beta functions and their porperties
  2. write down the power series expansion of functions

Textbooks

Required textbook:

  • G. B. Arfken and H. J. Weber, Mathematical Methods for Physicists, 6th Ed., Academic Press (1995)

Subsidiary books:

  • M. L. Boas, Mathematical Methods in the Physical Sciences, 2nd edition (1983)
  • Harper, Introduction to Mathematical Physics, Prentice-Hall, 4th ed.
