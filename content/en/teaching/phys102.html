Objectives

This course is the continuation of general physics I. In this course students will learns the fundamental concepts of waves, thermodynamics, electricity and magnetism.

Description of the course

Wave motion and sound, temperature, first and second law of thermodynamics, kinetic theory of gases, coulomb’s law, the electric field, Gauss’s law, electric potential, capacitors and dielectrics, D.C. circuits, the magnetic field, Ampere’s and Faraday’s laws.

Learning outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

  • define the characteristics of a wave
  • define physical quantities in thermodynamics
  • understand the kinetic theory of gases
  • understand and apply the first and second laws of thermodynamics
  • write down Coulomb's law and the properties of the electrostatic field
  • understand Gauss's law and use it to find electrostatic fields
  • define the electrostatic potential and its relation to electrostatic fields
  • appreciate the properties of capacitors and compute equivalent capacitance for capacitors in series and in parallel
  • identify the properties of current and resistance in circuits, as well as compute the equivalent resistances for resistors in series and in parallel
  • understand the junction rule and the loop rule (Kirchoff's law) for single and multiple circuits
  • know the properties of magnetic fields and how they may arise from currents in circuits
  • understand induction and compute inductance
