Objectives

The course provides a general introduction to the fundamental concepts of mechanics and fluids.

Description of the course

Particle kinematics and dynamics, conservation of energy and linear momentum, rotational kinematics, rigid body dynamics, conservation of angular momentum, simple harmonic motion, gravitation, the static and dynamics of fluids.
