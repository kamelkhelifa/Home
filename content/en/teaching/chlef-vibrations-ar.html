Objectives

To explore in detail vibrating systems and wave motion across many different physical systems.

Learning outcomes

On completion, successful students will be able to:

- demonstrate an understanding of the behaviour of vibrating systems and of wave motion;
- use the mathematical formalism that describes them;
- recognise examples in many areas of physics.

Content

1- Simple harmonic motion; energy in a vibrating system
2- Damped simple harmonic motion; the Q-value and power response curves
3- Forced simple harmonic motion; resonance and transients
4- Coupled simple harmonic motion; normal modes
5- Waves; the one-dimensional wave equation
6- Waves at interfaces; energy and power of a wave; standing waves
7- The wave equation in two and three dimensions; superposition
8- Phase velocity and group velocity; beats; dispersion
9- Interference and refraction
