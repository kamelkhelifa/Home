Syllabus

Course description

This course presents and explains the fundamental principles of quantum mechanics.

Learning outcomes

On completing the course, successful students will be able to:

  1. recognise some of the fundamental ideas and concepts of quantum mechanics;
  2. become familiar with the mathematical tools used in quantum mechanics;
  3. apply the skills acquired to solve a number of one-dimensional quantum problems;
  4. understand the postulates of quantum mechanics.
