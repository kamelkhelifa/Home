Syllabus

Course description

This course will integrate both thermal and statistical perspectives to discuss the general features and properties of equilibrium and simple models of gases, liquids, and solids.

Learning outcomes

Upon completion, successful students will be able to:

  1. demonstrate the ability to derive and discuss the three laws of thermodynamics.
  2. demonstrate the ability to use thermodynamic laws and relations to compute various thermodynamic quantities.
  3. derive the partition function for various ensembles and use it to compute thermodynamic quantities.
  4. study thermal properties of the ideal gas and other classical gases.
  5. demonstrate an understanding of thermodynamic properties of gases beyond the ideal gas.
  6. compute thermodynamic quantities and properties of various thermal systems.
