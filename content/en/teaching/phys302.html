Objectives

This course is a continuation of PHYS 301 (classical mechanics I) course. More advanced topics are introduced in classical mechanics

Description of the course

Topics covered motions of rigid bodies in three-dimensions, Euler's equation, gyroscopic motion, motion of a top, gyrocompass, inertia tensor, Lagrangian mechanics, generalised coordinates, Hamilton's variational principle, Hamiltonian equation, dynamics of oscillating systems, normal coordinates, coupled harmonic motion, continuous system and the wave equation.

Learning outcomes

Upon completion, successful students are expected to be able to:

  1. understand the dynamics of systems of particles
  2. apply classical laws to the motion of two interacting bodies
  3. solve problems involving collisions of particles
  4. compute the centre-of-mass and moment of inertia of a variety of rigid bodies
  5. compute dynamical variables for a rigid body undergoing a laminar motion

Syllabus

  1. Dynamics of systems of particles [FC: chap7] (Pdf)
    • Centre of mass and linear momentum of a system
    • Angular momentum and kinetic energy of a system
    • Motion of two interacting bodies
    • Collisions
    • Rocket motion
  2. Mechanics of rigid bodies [FC: chap8] (Pdf)
    • Centre of mass of a rigid body
    • Rotation of a rigid body about a fixed axis
    • Moment of Inertia
    • Physical pendulum
    • Angular momentum of a rigid body in laminar motion
    • Impulse and collisions of rigid bodies
  3. Motion of rigid bodies in 3D [FC: chap9]
    • Rotation about an arbitrary axis
    • Principal axes of a rigid body
    • Euler's equations
  4. Lagrangian mechanics [FC: chap10]
    • Hamilton's variational principle
