General Informations

  • Domain : SM (UEF22 / F224)
  • Semester : 4
  • Code : UEF224 (Phys4)
  • Title : Electromagnetism
  • Lectures : 1 session / week (1h30)
  • Tutorials : 1 session / week (1h30)
  • Assessment : Other (33%) + Exam (67%)
  • Prerequisites : UEF112 (Math3), UEF113 (Phys2)
  • Scholar year : 1437/38 - 2016/2017

Objectives:

Introducing Maxwell's equations and use them to derive the properties of electromagnetic waves ...

Learning outcome :

At the end of the course successful students will be able to:

  1. Describe the electric field, the electric potential, ...
  2. ... etc

Syllabus :

  1. Mathematical tools (Pdf)

    Analyse vectorielle (gradient, divergence, rotationnel et Laplacien); La distribution Delta de Dirac et les particules ponctuelles; Les équations de Poisson et Laplace.

  2. Electrostatic (Pdf)

    Coulomb's law

  3. Magnétostatic (Pdf)

    Magnetic field;

  4. Electrodynamics & Maxwell's equations (Pdf)

    Electromotive force,

  5. Propagation of EM waves (Pdf)

    Propagation of plane waves in vacuum,

  6. EM waves in materials (Pdf)

    Electrostatics in materials
