Syllabus

Course description

This course is continuation of PHYS 401: Quantum Mechanics I. It reviews previous materials with slightly more advanced techniques and introduces approximation methods that help in solving Schrodinger equation for complicated systems.

Learning outcomes

Upon completion, successful students will be able to:

  1. describe QM wave mechanics using Schrodinger equation
  2. demonstrate an understanding the underlying linear algebra in QM and implement in QM applications
  3. distinguish between Schrodinger and Heisenberg pictures of QM and be able to use either of them in solving QM problems
  4. demonstrate a through understanding of angular momentum and spin of particles and their addition
  5. use time-independent perturbation theory to understand QM systems and their properties.
