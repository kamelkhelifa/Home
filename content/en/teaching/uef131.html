Syllabus

Course description

This course is a continuation of Quantum Mechanics 1 and aims to deepen the understanding of the basic concepts of Quantum Mechanics 1 through applications to real quantum systems.

Learning outcomes

Upon completion, successful students will be able to:

  1. solve Schrödinger equation in one-dimension for various potential wells including the harmonic oscillator.
  2. solve Schrödinger equation in three-dimensions.
  3. demonstrate an understanding of the angular momentum in QM.
  4. utilise QM to understand central potential problems and describe the hydrogen atom.
  5. appreciate the spin of a particle.
  6. use approximation methods to solve complicated QM systems
