Course description

This is formally an English language course, targeted at physicists. There is however no detailed program that should be complied with. I decided to make this course look like "general physics" course in which we revise the physics courses taken by students from semester 1 up till semester 5. The revision is mostly descriptive given the fact that students have already seen the quantitative material. The courses covered are:

  1. Classical mechanics (S1)
  2. Electricity and magnetism (S2)
  3. Vibrations and Waves (S3)
  4. Electromagnetism (S4)
  5. Quantum mechanics (S4)
