Objectives:

To introduce Maxwell's equations and use them to derive the properties of electromagnetic waves, and to introduce simple models for the interaction of electromagnetic fields with matter.
Students will encounter wave equations of the same form as those they met in UEF123 (vibrations and waves).

Learning outcomes:

By the end of the course, successful students will be able to:

  1. describe the electric field and potential, and related concepts, for stationary charges.
  2. calculate the electrostatic properties of simple charge distributions using Coulomb's law, Gauss's law and the electric potential.
  3. describe the magnetic field for steady currents and moving charges.
  4. calculate the magnetic properties of simple current distributions using the Biot–Savart law and Ampère's law.
  5. describe electromagnetic induction and related concepts, and carry out calculations using Faraday's and Lenz's laws.
  6. state Maxwell's equations and demonstrate an understanding of them and of their derivation from the empirical laws of electromagnetism.
  7. apply the fundamental laws of electromagnetism to solve simple problems in electrostatics, magnetostatics and electromagnetic induction in vacuum.
  8. demonstrate an understanding of the properties of electromagnetic plane waves in vacuum and be able to derive these properties from Maxwell's equations.

Syllabus:

  1. Mathematical tools (Pdf)

    Vector analysis (gradient, divergence, curl and Laplacian); the Dirac delta distribution and point particles; Poisson's and Laplace's equations.

  2. Electrostatics (Pdf)

    Coulomb's law; the electric field; continuous charge distributions and Gauss's law; the electrostatic potential; Poisson's and Laplace's equations; work done by an electrostatic force; multipole expansion.

  3. Magnetostatics (Pdf)

    The magnetic field; the Lorentz force; the Biot–Savart law and Ampère's law; the magnetic vector potential; magnetic dipoles; multipole expansion.

  4. Electrodynamics and Maxwell's equations (Pdf)

    Electromotive force (emf); electromagnetic induction; Faraday's and Lenz's laws; inductance; energy in magnetic fields; the integral and differential forms of Maxwell's equations; the potential formulation.

  5. Propagation of electromagnetic waves (Pdf)

    Propagation of electromagnetic plane waves in vacuum; reflection and refraction: the laws of reflection and refraction; the Fresnel equations; guided waves: propagation along a straight line, coaxial lines, rectangular and hollow waveguides.

  6. EM in materials/media (Pdf1, Pdf2, Pdf3)

    Electrostatics in materials, magnetostatics in materials, Maxwell's equations and electromagnetic waves in materials.
