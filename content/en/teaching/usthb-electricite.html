Course description

Syllabus

  1. Electric interaction

    Electrification, conservation of charge, conductors and insulators, Coulomb's law

  2. Electrostatics

    - Field and potential produced by a charge, the superposition principle, charge distributions, field-line topography.
    - Electric dipoles: the potential and field produced by a dipole. Torque exerted on a dipole by a field. Energy of a dipole
    - The notion of flux, Gauss's theorem (without proof), applications.
    - Conductors in equilibrium, total influence.
    - Capacitors: capacitance, electrical energy, combinations of capacitors.
