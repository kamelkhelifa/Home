Objectives

  1. The study of vibrational phenomena with one or several degrees of freedom. The concept of impedance; the phenomenon of resonance.
  2. Highlighting the analogy between mechanical, electrical and electromechanical oscillators. Phase velocity, group velocity, dispersion. Characteristic impedance. The Doppler effect, which will be revisited within the framework of the theory of relativity.

Course description

Syllabus

  1. Part I: Vibrations

    1. Chapter 1: General properties of vibrations

      Definition of vibrational motion. Examples of vibrating systems. Periodic
      motion

    2. Chapter 2: Linear systems with one degree of freedom

      2.1 Free oscillations. The harmonic oscillator. Natural angular frequency of a harmonic
      oscillator. The energy of a harmonic oscillator
      2.2 Damped free oscillations. Damping forces. Equation of motion. Pseudo-periodic oscillations (logarithmic decrement, quality factor)
      2.3 Forced oscillations. Definition. The case of a sinusoidal driving force (resonance, phase shift). The case of an arbitrary periodic driving force. 2.4 Damped forced oscillations. Equation of motion. Transient regime, steady state. Bandwidth. Quality factor
      2.5 Analogy between mechanical and electrical oscillating systems

    3. Chapter 3: Linear systems with several degrees of freedom

      3.1 Systems with 2 degrees of freedom (free case: natural angular frequencies), damped, and damped and forced.
      3.2 Systems with N degrees of freedom (general behaviour)

  2. Part II: Waves

    1. Chapter 4: General properties of mechanical waves

      4.1 Classification of waves
      4.2 General solution of the general equation for travelling waves.
      4.3 Phase velocity, group velocity
      4.4 The notion of a wavefront. Examples: plane waves, spherical waves
      4.5 Reflection and transmission of waves
      4.6 Relations between the various quantities describing a wave

    2. Chapter 5: Transverse waves on a string

      5.1 Wave equation. Characteristic impedance. Energy of a travelling wave.
      Reflection and transmission of waves. Standing waves

    3. Chapter 6: Longitudinal waves in fluids

      6.1 Plane waves in a cylindrical pipe. Wave equation in a gas. Wave equation in a liquid. Acoustic impedance. Characteristic impedance. Energy carried by a wave. Reflection and transmission coefficients (boundary conditions)
      6.2 The Doppler effect

    4. Chapter 7: Elastic waves in solids
