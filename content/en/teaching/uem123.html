Syllabus

Course description

This course covers the essentials numerical methods that are widely used in physics/chemistry, as well as their implementation in efficient codes/programs

Textbooks for numerical methods:

  • [Fr] F. Jedrzeijewski, Introduction aux méthodes numériques, 2 ème Ed., Springer-Verlag, France, (2005).
  • [Fr] E. Hairer, Introduction à l’analyse numérique, université de Genève, (2001).
  • [En] J. Hoffman, Numerical methods for engineers and scientists, 2 nd Ed, Marcel Dekker, USA, (2001).
  • [Fr] A. Quarteroni, Méthodes numériques, algorithmes, analyse et applications, Springer-Verlag, Italie, (2004).

Subsidiary books:

  • [Matlab] C. Woodford and C. Phillips, Numerical methods with worked examples: MATLAB edition, 2nd Ed. Springer Ltd, (2013).
  • [Matlab] M. Djebli & H. Djelouah, Initiation à MATLAB, Office des Publications Universitaires OPU, (2013).
  • [Matlab] R. Dukkipati, MATLAB, an introduction with applications, New Age International Publishers, India, (2010).
  • [Matlab] R. B. Hahn and D. Valentine, Essential MATLAB for engineers and scientists, 3rd Ed., Elsevier Ltd, (2007).
  • [C/C++] C. Casteyde, Cours de C/C++, Copyright, (2005).
  • [C/C++] C. Delannoy, C++ pour les programmeurs C, 6ème Ed., Eyrolles, Paris, (2004).
  • [Fortran] P. Corde et A. Fouilloux, Langage Fortran, Support de cours, IDRIS, (2010).
  • [Fortran] P. D’Anfray, Fortran 77, Université Paris XIII, (1998).
  • [Fortran] B. Hahn, Introduction to Fortran 90 for scientists and engineers, Capetown University, South Africa, (1993).
