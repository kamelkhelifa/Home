Syllabus

Course description

This course is continuation of PHYS 401: Quantum Mechanics I. It reviews previous materials with slightly more advanced techniques and introduces approximation methods that help in solving Schrodinger equation for complicated systems.

Learning outcomes

Upon completion, successful students will be able to:

  1. describe QM wave mechanics using Schrodinger equation
  2. demonstrate a through understanding of angular momentum and spin of particles and their addition
  3. demonstrate an understanding of the concepts and techniques used for systems of identical particles
  4. use time-independent perturbation theory to understand QM systems and their properties.
  5. apply the variational principles to determine approximate values for ground energy of complex systems
  6. apply the WKB approximation to obtain approximate solutions to Schrodinger equation of complex systems
