Objectives

To introduce:

  1. The basic concepts of mechanics: mass (inertia and attraction), force, energy, field, potential, momentum, angular momentum.
  2. Conservation laws and their applications.

Course description

Syllabus

  1. Mathematical review

    Dimensional equations, error calculation, vectors

  2. Kinematics of a point particle

    Rectilinear motion, curvilinear motion, analysis in different coordinate systems, relative motion

  3. Dynamics of a point mass

    The principle of inertia, momentum, Newton's laws, the laws of gravitation, frictional forces, elastic forces, angular momentum

  4. Work and energy

    Work done by a force, kinetic energy, potential energy (gravitational and elastic forces), mechanical energy, the law of conservation of energy, field and potential: the cases of weight and gravitation

  5. Collisions between two isolated particles

    Elastic collisions, inelastic collisions
