Syllabus

Formally an English language course, I have turned it into an introductory course in particle physics. It mainly provides a qualitative description of the basics of particle physics, such as the fundamental interactions (forces) and elementary building blocks of matter. The material presented in the lectures is based on that delivered by Prof. Fred Leobinger at the University of Manchester.

Also presented in the lectures was an introduction to the type-setting program "Latex". Full details may be found in my post "A Physicist's Toolkit".

There will be one final exam (weighting 100%) at the end of the semester.

The main topics covered are:

  1. Introduction
  2. Interactions
  3. Conservation laws
